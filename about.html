Our story, our purpose.

For more than two decades, Extreme High School has been shaping confident young leaders.

Since 2001

A legacy of growth and belief

Extreme High School began with a simple conviction: every young person deserves a remarkable education. What started as a small community school has grown into a diverse, high-achieving learning community in Nairobi.

We remain grounded in personal attention, high standards and a genuine partnership with families. Our learners leave ready to contribute thoughtfully and courageously wherever life takes them.

Mission & vision

Our missionTo inspire excellence, character and lifelong learning in every student.
Our visionTo be a leading African school developing globally minded change-makers.

Our core values

Excellence. We pursue our best in all that we do.   Integrity. We act with honesty and respect.   Innovation. We are curious, brave and resourceful.   Service. We use our gifts to strengthen others.
